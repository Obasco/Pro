About Me

My Bio

Hello! I’m Olumide Obayemi, a passionate front-end developer and creative thinker from Nigeria. I’ve always been fascinated by how design and code come together to create meaningful user experiences. My journey into tech started out of curiosity — wanting to understand how websites work — and since then, I’ve been consistently learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.

Beyond code, I’m deeply interested in self-improvement, fitness, and helping others learn and grow. I see technology as more than a career — it’s a tool for solving problems, expressing creativity, and shaping the future.

My Goals in the HNG Program

  • Build real-world, production-ready front-end projects that show my growth and skills.
  • Strengthen my understanding of JavaScript, React, and clean code practices.
  • Collaborate effectively in teams and contribute meaningfully to group projects.
  • Develop a strong portfolio and prepare myself for global tech opportunities.

Areas Where I Need More Confidence

While I’m improving rapidly, I’m still working on becoming more confident in:

  • Advanced React patterns like custom hooks and state management with Redux or Context API.
  • Backend logic — especially when integrating APIs efficiently.
  • Debugging complex UI bugs quickly under pressure.
  • Presenting my ideas clearly in large collaborative teams.

Extra Thoughts & Reflections
